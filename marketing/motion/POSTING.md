# Plutto motion series — posting kit

Ten vertical films (1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264), made for Instagram Reels,
YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Each has a cover frame (`*-cover.jpg`) for the
upload screen. They are silent by design: add a trending or licensed track
inside each app — platforms favour their own audio, and it keeps the films
free of music licensing.

**Order.** Lead with 01 (the brand film), then alternate: one education
film, one sky-calendar film, one product film. The sky-calendar films are
dated — post 04 before 24 Oct 2026, 05 any time before 2 Aug 2027, 06 in the
weeks before 3 Jun 2027.

Every date in these films comes from Swiss Ephemeris via the site's data
(`app/lib/data/ephemeris.json`) — the same engine the app uses.
